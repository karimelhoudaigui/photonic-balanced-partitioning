## What survives

For N=2k, EVEN k>=2, two independent color-resolved pure zero-displacement
Gaussian sources with real pair matrix Z=lambda W, W=J-I+epsilon A,
||Z||<1, the raw probability of red CF S and blue CF complement is

    q(S)=det(I-lambda^2 W^2)*lambda^(2k)*haf(W_S)^2*haf(W_Sc)^2.

There are2N resolved degrees of freedom. Vacuum, other number sectors,
collisions and invalid colors all remain failures. The determinant appears
once after multiplying TWO colors; one Gaussian alone has its square root.
Odd k is forbidden by each color's parity. This is a changed active source,
not an implementation of the old fixed-photon passive proposal for free.

At fixed graph and size, the accepted mean cut has derivative
`-2 Var_uniform(C)/(k-1)` at epsilon0. An incoherent matching weight has
the SAME derivative. More strongly, an explicit polynomial feasible sampler
mixes uniform balanced draws with weighted-edge-UNCUT-conditioned balanced
draws and reproduces the exact affine first-order density whenever
`0<=2epsilon*total_edge_weight/(2k-1)<=1`. It needs neither optimum nor
hafnian evaluation; its actual generation/scoring costs remain paid.

The first relative coherent log term is centered matching variance,
`-epsilon^2[(V_S+V_Sc)-E0(V_S+V_Sc)]`. V is computable in O(k^2) from
induced edge sums, squared sums and row sums. The correction has no guaranteed
favorable sign; k=2 has identical coherent/incoherent conditional weights
at every strength. A cheap truncated score does not prove an efficient
exact sampler, uniform remainder or finite-strength optimization theorem.
The matching mixture is a combinatorial control, not an authenticated fully
distinguishable Gaussian physical law.

At epsilon0 ONLY, the exact raw acceptance has a uniform-lambda ceiling
`min(1,e^2*k^4*exp(-2k))`; accepted records per mean emitted photon are at
most `(e^2/2)*k^4*exp(-2k)`. Both colors' mean counts include all number
sectors. These background bounds cannot be extrapolated to every finite
epsilon, every graph or all Gaussian sources.

A full nonzero Gaussian has unbounded photon support, so finite-Fock input
with passive optics/vacuum/count heralds cannot prepare it exactly. Separately,
heralding m-2 photons from m distinct CF input photons leaves a pure pair
matrix of rank<=m. Generic rank-N pair preparation requires m>=N in THAT
model, a necessary condition without a sufficiency or success-rate claim.
Fixed-number substitutes need their own normalization and preparation budget.

The two versioned primary papers authenticate known Gaussian-hafnian and
dense-subgraph ingredients; their bounded check is not novelty evidence.
No simulation, physical distinguishability benchmark, held-out opening,
manuscript revision, hardware freeze or QPU call follows from this result.
