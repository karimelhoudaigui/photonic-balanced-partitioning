## A. Residual cut, frame, and raw pair contrast

Assume an undirected loopless graph, symmetric weights w, S0=S\R, and distinct Y disjoint from S0.
Adding one j removes its edges to S0 from the cut and adds its edges to the old complement.

Thus

    C(S0 union Y)-C(S0) = sum_(j in Y) d_j - 2 sum_(j<h in Y) w_jh,

with d_j exactly as (1). Edges outside the receiver pool still contribute to d_j.
For |Y|=|R|, lifting preserves the original balance. No optimum or C* enters this identity.

    E_B[n_j n_h] = mu_j mu_h + |g_jh|² - 2 s_jh,

    <n,f| Gamma(V_f directsum I) P_f Gamma(W_G) |input>
