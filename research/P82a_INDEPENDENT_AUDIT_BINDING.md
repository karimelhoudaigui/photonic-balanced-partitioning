## Finite checker behavior

After raw seal and separate authorization, the checker may reconstruct
only registered classical graphs and marks. It checks B scores including
repeated neighbors, own reset/current semantics, best-not-last Q, 96
attempts/768 completed modeled photons, all nine r bins, full16 counts,
exact split integers and repair branches. F4 Q/A/J are independently
derived with Fraction complex arithmetic. H/zero/atan2 use ARCHIVED
rounded J and preceding phasors in the frozen order: a theoretical zero
cannot replace a nonzero recorded field. Fixed numerical residual is
3e-12 absolute, relative0; H zero and clock comparisons have no tolerance.

Each control mask gets a new scalar cut. Select the latest existing
dual-timely checkpoint. Direct P50 prefixes are diagnostic; conservative
gap-charged prefixes decide. Preserve first-scan/early-guard no-winner
inconclusiveness. A SAME fixed mode must strictly beat B on BOTH anchors;
all normal Tier1 calls and B attempts are required. Tier2 has all eight
registered calls only on its declared branch. Failure precedes a partial
veto, optional work remains NOT_RUN and positive_accepted is always false.

No Clifford, Naive, full-Fock or P50 solver replay occurs. A pass concerns
recorded finite protocol consistency, not Born law, physical photons,
TV, advantage or independent quantum replication. Malformed/hash-invalid
inputs, recorded mismatches and failed/incomplete primary prefixes have
distinct statuses. Partial evidence never becomes an accepted veto.
