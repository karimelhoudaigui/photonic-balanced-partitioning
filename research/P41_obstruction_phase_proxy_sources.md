# P41/R3 — exact source overlap for the phase proxy

Primary checked: Hayashi, Markham, Murao, Owari, Virmani,
[*Entanglement of multiparty stabilizer, symmetric, and antisymmetric states*,
arXiv0710.1056v2](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.1056v2),16-Jan-2008;
Phys.Rev.A77,012104. Exact §III.2 Eqs(32)-(40) read24-Nov-2026.

Dictionary: source n=k particles, d>=k single-particle levels, composition
(1,...,1,0,...). Its fixed-type symmetric basis state is the distinct-input
Fock state in first quantization. Eqs(33)-(34) give optimal squared product
overlap k!/k^k. Eq(40)'s local phase twirl of a symmetric product has that
state as a positive summand of exactly this weight. Passive U acts as U^tensor-k;
PNR measurement of each Hartree component is multinomial. Hence the continuous
phase-iid optical distribution dominates (k!/k^k)P_B pointwise.

Overlap verdict: **formal corollary not stated** for this optical probability
comparison; coefficient and operator/twirling mechanism are **known after
translation**. Do not claim either as a newly discovered mathematical principle.
The finite-sign construction, without-replacement strengthening, loss/cost
application and concrete graph controls require their separate proofs and
source checks. A particle-product Hartree state is not a claim of a positive
Glauber-P mode-classical state.

For pure product internal states, the orthogonal single-particle levels can
be |input a>tensor|phi_a>: distinct spatial inputs make them orthogonal.
The same dictionary then applies, tracing unobserved internal degrees after U.
Its classical sampling implementation needs accessible Gram/intensity data;
arbitrary opaque correlated internal states do not inherit that implementation.

Adjacent primary inspected: Opanchuk, Rosales-Zarate, Reid, Drummond,

representations*, Phys.Rev.A97,042304](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevA.97.042304),

estimators and relates discrete phases to Gurvits; it explicitly distinguishes
estimating correlations from generating exact permanental counts. **Partial
or adjacent result only** for a positive normalized proposal dominating useful
event probabilities; do not call an estimator an exact boson sampler.
