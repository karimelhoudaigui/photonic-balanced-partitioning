## Checked primary sources and exact scope

1. Changhun Oh, *Classical simulability of constant-depth linear-optical
   circuits with noise*, npj Quantum Information11:126(2025),
   [publisher PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-025-01041-w.pdf),
   DOI10.1038/s41534-025-01041-w. Theorem1 and Corollary1 on pp3-4 were read.
   It proves approximate classical sampling under a light-cone-degree/loss
   condition; its quarter-survival corollary is **per beam-splitter layer**.
   It is not our end-to-end cardinality/search envelope. Verdict: **partial
   or adjacent result only**, not a source proving or refuting (A)-(D).

2. Solomons, Thomas, McCutcheon,
   [arXiv2301.13217v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.13217v1),30-Jan-2023,
   *Gaussian-boson-sampling-enhanced dense subgraph finding shows limited
   advantage over efficient classical algorithms*. AppendixF was read in this
   exact version. It counts all draws and discusses discarding wrong click
   counts, while retuning squeezing for each loss level. A bounded fresh photon
   source is not its assumption. Verdict: **partial or adjacent result only**.
   This version is not conflated with the differently titled published paper.

3. Mills and Mezher,
   [arXiv2405.02278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02278), associated
   [Quantum2026 publication](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-03-16-2030).
   Abstract/public summary inspected; full original theorem not audited here.
   It constructs mitigated probability/mean estimators from lossy statistics,
   not additional genuine detected-photon candidate samples. Its estimation
   task differs from (A1). Verdict: **partial or adjacent result only**;
   this does not dismiss mitigation for estimation or hybrid algorithms.
