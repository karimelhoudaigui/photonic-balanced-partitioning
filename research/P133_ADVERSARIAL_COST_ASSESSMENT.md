## What the small discriminator can actually establish

With r6,k4 there are exactly 15 receiver CF sets. Evaluating all their lifted
cuts solves that entire residual pool classically, including its best hold
or improvement. Its graph may have 24 vertices; the post-feature choice still
has 15 candidates. The same complete pool/frontier must be credited to the
classical comparator. A biased optical sample cannot beat the best known
pool member in cut quality. This says nothing about other receiver pools.

The physical qualification can still ask whether the registered compiler
transfers four-input interference into RAW graph utility. Use
J_pi(theta)=sum_Y p_pi(Y;theta)*[C(S)-C(S0 union Y)]_+,
with every dump, loss and collision assigned zero utility and fully paid.
There is no survival denominator and no optimal-state mask. A positive
same-T B/D contrast is weaker than an improvement over tuned controls.

For a fixed, fully disclosed finite grid, a stronger interference witness is
max_theta J_B(theta)>max_(pi proper,theta) J_pi(theta), by an exact positive
margin including approximation error if any. This compares only that grid
and those compilers, not continuous-family optima or algorithmic complexity.
It does not by itself demonstrate a uniquely four-cycle mechanism, rather
than an interaction of several exchange orders. Preserve all grid values,
zeros and reversals; a favorable isolated point remains an isolated witness.
Even this witness loses the r6 optimization comparison to its solved frontier.

## Four-photon controls and the prototype endpoint

There are 14 proper input-species partitions: one 1+1+1+1, six 2+1+1,
three 2+2, and four 3+1. Source indices matter; testing only one grouping
per block-size pattern is insufficient. Each partition independently selects
its beta,kappa, admissible scale and every prototype/mark option available
to B, with the same declared family and paid training resources.

At beta=kappa=0, the P133 compiler sends one photon to each prototype receiver
with unit ideal signal transmission. Thus every allowed prototype tuple has
a D/group endpoint preparing that KNOWN candidate, without interference.
It must be scored/credited, including prototype tuples examined and rejected
during design. Restricting D to B's selected nonzero setting or withholding
this endpoint manufactures a contrast. Conversely, allowing D an optimum
oracle or an unbudgeted arbitrary compiler would not be a matched comparison.
The classical baseline can simply return an already scored prototype; its
preparation is not an optical optimization discovery. Hardware attenuation
and setting costs remain separate measured quantities for both instruments.

## Sampling cost is not receiver-subset enumeration

[Clifford and Clifford, 1706.01260v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.01260v2),
Theorem 1 and Algorithm B, give arithmetic cost O(k*2^k+m*k^2) per full
boson sample and O(m) additional space. The correctness argument uses
orthonormal occupied columns; it does not require a new Haar draw per sample.
For a structured frame retain the uniform column permutation: the optional
Haar-only omission in the final remark is unavailable. These are arithmetic
counts, not measured wall time, a bit-cost theorem or an implemented control.

Apply the exact-law control to the complete m=r+k passive unitary and then
the SAME CF/hold map. Sampling dumps/collisions is part of an attempt; there
is no rejection-until-CF factor. Thus r^k or binom(r,k) enumeration is not an
unavoidable price of reproducing B. Time a reviewed implementation with its
input construction, precision and score costs; never substitute naive
enumeration as the sole exact-law baseline. External noise needs its stated
model and cannot be inferred from this ideal-unitary transfer.

## The actual r12 research gate and the leakage traps

The changed r12,k4 question has 495 feasible candidates, not a hardness claim.
Share residual d_j,w_jh and all preprocessing with FAST/pair/beam/frontier
search, all 14 retuned species families and the exact-law sampler. Permit
all 495 scores whenever their measured grant allows; do not hide a frontier
because the optical method used fewer reported output scores. A useful
next gate needs a frozen graph-dependent policy on fresh residuals/graphs,
and measured improvement per TOTAL setup/training/attempt/score cost over
these controls. Ambient n, photons, wall time and cut evaluations are
different resource ledgers; none may be silently exchanged for another.

Computing exact J over all states for parameter selection is an expensive
training oracle, not a few-shot learner. Charge it to both families or use
it only for qualification. Likewise, evaluating 15/495 cuts to select marks,
then presenting only subsequent draws, hides classical feasible search.
Do not tune on held-out graph raw laws/cuts, select graphs after seeing the
contrast, or recycle an inspected graph as an unexposed test. Retrospective
optima may evaluate a result; they cannot guide its training/encoding.

raw witness survives these controls; it cannot pass the optimizer gate.
