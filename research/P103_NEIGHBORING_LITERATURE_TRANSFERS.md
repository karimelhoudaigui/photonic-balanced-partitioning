Current ranking uses qualitative knowledge, not invented scores/probabilities:
positive transport has high tractability but no new hard interference claim;
arc-walk has a concrete structural bridge but unproved multiphoton benefit
and mode/relabel costs; DPP is presently a strong CONTROL rather than a
bosonic candidate; hafnian density is established adjacent work, with a
missing cut/device bridge. None clears performance/replication gates.
P100/P101 remain genuinely different live sources of nonlinear/temporal
mechanisms; P105 will rank extra candidates with their first falsifiers.
