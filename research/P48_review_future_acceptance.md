# P48 — prospective confirmation gates (no execution authorized)

This plan applies ONLY if frozen P48 DEV produces a credible signal. Four DEV
graphs cannot accept a positive. This document launches no cycle, generates no
graph, opens no held-out outcome and changes no P48 code or qualified helper.

## 1. Freeze before opening held-out data

Across candidates, reserve alpha_j=.05/[j(j+1)] for confirmation candidate j>=1;
the entire program sums to.05. Split alpha_j equally between H1/H2, then among
their declared comparisons. Record j, source/protocol hashes, seed commitments
and allocated alpha in an append-only ledger BEFORE its first confirmation
panel is generated/opened. A simple DEV attempt consumes no j. An opened panel
is never confirmation data for a later candidate; failure does not refund alpha
or silently reset it to.05. New candidates need fresh unopened panels.

Commit the complete B/D/b4/Gaussian/IID/phase/sphere policies SEPARATELY:
encoder, decoder lambda, precision/corrections, source groups, marks/order rules,
frontend, stopping rules and all training costs. A size-dependent policy is
allowed only if declared for every control before the test. No C*, optimum
mask, optimum partition or outcome-selected graph enters any stage.

Freeze efficient classical controls too: vectorized/incremental annealing,
spectral multistart + local search, collective KL, shared-Delta block matching,
and coherent rounding + feasible search. Include exact classical B sampling as
a costed competitor where available, and a separately frozen B-Fourier ablation.
All controls may exploit the explicit graph and their full CPU allowance;
do not force them to use a photon-like proposal count or unneeded preprocessing.

## 2. The resource gate is mandatory

**Informative score units alone, and optical-simulator time, cannot pass this
gate.** On an explicit graph all K² swap deltas can be formed in one array as
ell_out-ell_in+2A_cross. Classical search must use efficient incremental updates,
vectorized scans and reasonable multistart tuning, rather than recomputing every
cut in Python. No per-candidate JSON logging belongs in the timed kernel.
Use compact traces outside timing, or separately measured I/O; verify the fast
kernel against audited references before held-out runs. Archive the old pilot
frontiers as DEV evidence, not as hardware/resource bounds.

Record two ledgers: total experimental spending and each deployable policy's
cost. Count its upfront training, per-graph preparation, all objective-estimation
shots, rejected/lost attempts, decoding, calibration and parameter selection.
Do not amortize training over infinitely many graphs: the primary campaign is
the actual retained test panel. Cache rules are fixed and shared fairly.
Classical controls may bypass unnecessary photonic preparation. As an additional
conservative test they may receive the same public Cref for free and ignore it
if a different restart is better; B's actual cost of obtaining it stays charged.

Before crediting any optical latency, require B to beat efficient classical
search allowed at least B's OBSERVED classical preparation+tuning+decoder CPU.
Allow D/coherent samplers to generate as many proposals as their CPU permits.
Run precommitted longer classical prefixes to measure time-to-match B's cut.
Report the residual optical allowance = observed time-to-match minus B's
classical CPU, with censoring if no match is observed. This is a break-even
condition, not a device latency prediction. Source/configuration/calibration/
loss/retry/readout/queue costs must eventually fit that allowance. A baseline
already matching within B's classical overhead vetoes a total-resource claim.

## 3. Reserved held-out panel and statistical unit

Proposed panel H1:128 NEW graphs,64 at each r13/17 (N26/34); within each size
ER(p=.35) versus RR(degree4) is an independent fair family choice. Weights1..9.
Keep every graph. Fewer than16 examples of either family/size makes coverage
inconclusive; never replace a graph to fix that. Two fresh mark/order sets,
each with native and carried-permutation labels, and two fresh sampling seeds
give8 paired batches per graph. Default32 B attempts per batch at its frozen
policy; CPU-based controls retain their actual full time allowances.
Carry marks, priorities, source orders and vertex attributes in the relabel
test. Fresh marks/orders are a DIFFERENT robustness test, not a trivial rename.

The primary unit is the GRAPH: average batch results within each graph first.
Paired cuts from one trajectory, seeds, relabels and photon samples are not new
independent graphs. A zero-gain or Cref=0 graph remains a tie in the denominator.
No optional stopping, seed search, silent timeout exclusion or post-test retuning.
Unexpected missing/numerically invalid cases block acceptance; planned budget
stops return the verified best prefix. Preserve failures and all old outcomes.

For each comparator define a graph win when B's batch-averaged best feasible
cut is at least ONE weight unit lower, a loss for the converse, otherwise a tie.
Report pooled and size-specific wins/losses/ties plus ALL cost differences.
For each size, graphs are iid under the declared family mixture and independent
randomness. With n=64, use simultaneous one-sided exact binomial bounds for
P(win) and P(loss), retaining ties in n. For C=13 declared comparisons, allocate
stage alpha=alpha_j/2 over2*C*2 bounds (two sizes, two probabilities). Require
lower(P(win))-upper(P(loss))>0 for EVERY comparator at BOTH sizes; the equally
weighted pooled lower bound follows. Zero wins or all ties cannot pass.
If the comparator list changes, freeze its new multiplicity budget beforehand.

Also report paired graph means/medians and stratified graph-cluster bootstrap
intervals (fixed seed,199999 resamples; same family correction). These intervals
are approximate; they do not prove distribution-free expected-cost dominance.
Require their mean differences to be positive too, to reject a win-count story
with observed large losses. The formal probability claim remains win-versus-loss
under the declared population, not arbitrary-graph or asymptotic superiority.

## 4. Independent replication before hardware design

H1 passing only creates a candidate. Require NEW actual physical samples from
an independently authored count simulator/representation, not reaggregation,
a new wrapper of the same backend, or only agreement of reported summaries.
Check small full-Fock laws, collisions, isometries, PMFs and covariances first;
then repeat r13/17 using a fresh panel H2 of128 graphs and the same gates,
without refitting the frozen policies. Separately reproduce a fixed H1 subset
with fresh counts for implementation agreement. Raw counts/commands/seeds and
software/precision provenance must be archived. Uncertified truncation or an
unaffordable independent sampler makes this gate INCONCLUSIVE, not replicated.
H2 receives its sealed alpha_j/2 budget; no claims are selected between panels.

## 5. What may be claimed; hardware remains a separate gate

After both panels, the permitted claim is a reproducible simulated improvement
over the NAMED independently tuned controls under the declared graph population,
CPU policy and photonic-attempt budget. The exact win-probability statement,
empirical mean quality and conditional optical break-even envelope are separate.
This is neither a speedup inferred from simulator timing nor a theorem against
all classical algorithms. A spectral-encoding contribution additionally needs
the graph-blind ablation; fewer collisions or better raw means are insufficient.

After H2 passes, update paper/TeX/PDF and GitHub with ONLY the validated simulated
results and explicitly mark hardware validation PENDING. Preserve all negatives;
do not imply that simulation is QPU confirmation. Then design the authorized
hardware stage. If r13/17 does not fit the
actual platform, shrinking it is a NEW size-transfer experiment: independently
confirm the mechanism and controls at that size before freezing the circuit.
Do not load a known solution or substitute HOM alone for optimization validation.
Record actual platform/modes/source/detector/loss/internal-state capabilities;
charge physical attempts, calibration/training, compilation, failures and all
classical work. Empty shots remain counted. Marginal PIT must use the declared
loss/noise law or separately qualified calibration, never favorable postselection.
Prepare Laurent's reproducible branch/commands only after these gates; any QPU
job still needs its explicit authorization. A later hardware result is a
separate article update. For now the paper remains frozen: no positive exists.
