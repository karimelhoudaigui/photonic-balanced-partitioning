# P55 recorded-data audit: plan frozen before reading the outcome

Target: the one run of the final P55 source
`767b7c65fd240e704ded00654f88fbc3b5af3e8d`, SHA256
`f5563a868e6464e39a669d1fdecfb213374d203d0bf91fea64b7de6d69d422d7`.
The source and declared JSON schema are known; the reviewer has not read
the main raw, summary or outcome when writing this plan. Prior participation
in protocol/source review is disclosed. This is an independent audit of
recorded data and accounting, not a new physical replication or a blind
choice of the hypothesis.

Prepare a compact standalone checker. Never import the primary or any of
its helper modules. The mathematical/data checks use stdlib integer,
Fraction, hash, gzip, JSON and Random operations. NumPy/NetworkX may be
used solely to reproduce the two already fixed graph recipes with their
pinned algorithms; this replays existing graph seeds and generates no new
instance or optical/control observation. No scientific calculation runs
until source, actual input closure and resource contract are frozen,
reviewed, and ROOT allocates an exclusive worker.

Checks, retaining failures and incomplete populations:

1. Verify supplied graph records against the two fixed seeded ER/RR
   recipes, sorted-edge PCG64 integer weights, symmetry, zero diagonal,
   integer weights and context copies. Verify case/source dimensions and
   all master/observation/rank/phase/group/FAST seed formulas.
2. For each successful raw shot, recompute the integer cut and balanced
   feasibility. Independently expand `prod_i(1+x*z**n_i)` using sparse
   coefficient dictionaries; find all minimizing photon sums and their
   exact multiplicities. Verify ways, target counts, imbalance and DP bound.
   Invert the declared subset ranking using coefficient queries on prefix
   products, instead of invoking or copying the production unranking.
   Verify the saved rank equals that inverse and the successive private
   `Random(seed_rank).randrange(ways)` values within each batch. This checks
   the recorded PRNG protocol, not physical randomness or uniformity from
   finitely many observations. Preserve variable-M product events.
3. Match every complete batch to its raw successful shots, all cut sums,
   exact normalized means, best subsets, role seeds and expected population.
   For failures, check the recorded prefix and failure evidence without
   inventing a completed batch or replacing a missing draw.
4. Recompute all policy Fraction losses from all shots of that policy,
   equal graph averaging and canonical ties; require selected fresh policies
   match selection.json and summary. The raw stream must have no training
   after fresh begins. The static source establishes that selection.json is
   written before fresh; JSON's assertion alone does not prove wall-clock
   chronology, immutability during execution, or a new independent trial.
5. Recompute per-model train/fresh/paid incumbents and all fresh means,
   especially all80 B observations on a completed run. Validate cached
   online best against all completed observations, accounting for earliest
   ties. Recompute every B allowance from B-only preparation, caller setup,
   postprocessing, aggregation and selection share. Require optical backend
   WALL equal the saved immutable-factory report scope, factory prefix paid,
   total factory clocks retained and unavailable CPU split explicitly None.
   Saved clocks cannot independently prove physical timing or omitted work.
6. Validate FAST subsets/cuts, nullable eligible snapshots, deadline and
   first-scan eligibility, cap flags, adapter subtraction and per-mode budget.
   Recompute the exact fixed-algorithm veto, required D/CTMC global-mean veto,
   strict every-control/every-graph follow-up gate and final status. Missing
   or failed work remains inconclusive; positive acceptance is always false.

Include small synthetic guards for sparse coefficient counts/rank inversion,
odd/all-zero/variable totals, cut corruption and verdict cases, without
sampling physics. The audit reports every limitation and never upgrades
`FOLLOW_UP_ONLY` to advantage, held-out validation or hardware feasibility.
